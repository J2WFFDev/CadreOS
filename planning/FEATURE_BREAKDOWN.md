# CadreOS Feature Breakdown

## 1) Identity, People, and Relationships
- User accounts and profiles
- Role model (athlete, parent, coach, staff, admin)
- Relationship links (athlete-parent, athlete-coach, multi-role users)

## 2) Program and Team Management
- Programs, teams, seasons
- Rosters and staff assignments
- Group visibility boundaries

## 3) Notes and Development Records
- Quick notes from mobile
- Tagged observations (skill, behavior, logistics, wellness)
- Historical timeline per athlete/team
- Athlete journal entries as first-class records (freeform and prompt-driven)

## 4) Communication and Routing
- Inbox for triage
- Role-aware message routing
- Controlled visibility and escalation

## 5) Event Scheduling and Calendar
- Practices, games, meetings, travel events
- Assignment by team/group
- RSVP and availability tracking

## 6) Attendance and Participation
- Check-in/check-out and attendance statuses
- Absence reasons
- Participation trend visibility

## 7) Tasks and Workflows
- Follow-up task creation from notes/messages/events
- Owners, due dates, status tracking
- Reminder and overdue views

## 8) Athlete Development and Performance
- Goals and milestones
- Development plans
- Progress snapshots
- Reflection journaling lifecycle (draft/final/archive)
- Prompt library with assignment/scheduling and completion tracking
- Guardian-aware athlete development feed visibility

## 9) Health, Safety, and Compliance
- Incident and safety logging
- Consent/form status tracking
- Compliance acknowledgment tracking

## 10) Inventory and Asset Management
- Equipment catalog
- Assignment and return tracking
- Condition status

## 11) Reporting and Analytics
- Attendance trends
- Task throughput
- Communication response metrics
- Development progress summaries

## 12) Automation and Intelligence
- Rule-based notifications
- Suggested follow-ups from records
- Priority surfacing in inbox
