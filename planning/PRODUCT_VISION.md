# CadreOS Product Vision

## Product Name
CadreOS

## Product Category
Athlete Program Operating System

## Vision Statement
CadreOS is a mobile-first operating system for amateur and professional athlete programs that unifies people, communication, scheduling, execution, and long-term athlete development into one secure, role-aware platform.

## Problem
Programs currently operate across fragmented tools (chat apps, spreadsheets, forms, email, and point solutions), creating communication gaps, missed follow-ups, and weak organizational memory.

## Target Users
- Athletes
- Parents/guardians
- Coaches
- Program directors and administrators
- Medical, performance, and support staff

## Core Outcomes
- Faster and clearer communication
- Better accountability and follow-through
- Higher participation and attendance reliability
- Better visibility into athlete development progress
- Reduced operational overhead for staff

## Product Principles
1. Mobile-first capture and action
2. Role-based visibility and permissions
3. Structured records plus flexible notes
4. Workflow before complexity
5. Auditability and trust by default
